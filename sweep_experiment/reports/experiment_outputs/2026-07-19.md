  smoke-tested:**

     have lost every SAVi video for the figure bank / future experiments (the

     is self-contained and never touched the shared VBench harness, so it had a
     different metric set than the AdaSteer runs.
- **Quality caveat (NOT yet resolved):** PSNR 8.35 / SSIM 0.05 / LPIPS 0.85 are
  very poor. Partly expected for a smoke (6 Euler steps, 2 rollout steps, and the
  differentiable sampler runs CFG-off), but SSIM ~0.05 is low enough that SAVi-DNO
  must be validated at production settings (>=10 Euler / >=10 rollout) AND against
  its own `--no-optimize` LongCat baseline on the same clips before we trust it as
  a paper baseline. If `--no-optimize` scores similarly low, the culprit is the
  undercooked/CFG-off generation, not the noise optimization.
