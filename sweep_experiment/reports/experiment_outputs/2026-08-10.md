**Action taken:**
- `diag_longhorizon_drift.py`: added `--method ttc|ttc_gated` on the
  `SAViDNO_LongCat`+`TTC_LongCat` engine. Knobs: `--ttc-sigma-threshold` (0.3),
  `--ttc-cadence` (1), `--ttc-weight` (0.1), `--ttc-full-latent`,
  `--ttc-gate-threshold` (0.15). Per chunk: encode the in-memory conditioning tail
  -> `cond_latents`; sample with `return_latents=True`; DECODE the full
  `[cond|gen]` latent stack JOINTLY so frame geometry matches the pipeline

  incoming_drift}` per chunk.
