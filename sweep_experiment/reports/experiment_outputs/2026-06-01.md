**Top priority:** correlation plot of metric performance vs video dynamicness, all
methods (NoTTA, AdaSteer, LoRA-R8, TinyLoRA) on the same graph, per dataset.

**Metric choice (recommended):** mean RAFT optical-flow magnitude — the continuous
score underlying VBench's "Dynamic Degree" (Huang et al., CVPR 2024). Same flow
basis is used by SVD, AnimateDiff, ModelScopeT2V, CogVideoX, VBench++. Recognizable
to reviewers, single citation suffices.

**Analysis design:**
- x-axis: per-video mean RAFT flow magnitude of the GT eval clip (intrinsic
  difficulty, same x for all methods)
- y-axis: per-video PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS (3 panels)
- 5 quintile bins (~200 vids/bin), mean ± SEM error bars
- 4 lines per axis: NoTTA / ADA / LORA_R8_TTA / TL_BARE_R2 (TinyLoRA where merged)
- Expected if "TTA overfits high-motion" hypothesis is true: low-motion bins
  cluster together, TTA curves drop below NoTTA in high-motion bins ("X" shape)

**Pipeline written locally (to be pushed):**
- `scripts/compute_dynamic_degree.py` — RAFT-small via torchvision (Farneback fallback);
  output JSON: `{video_id: {mean_flow, max_flow, n_frame_pairs, h, w}}`
