## What is *missing* from this picture

1. **`panda_1000v_retrieval/*`** — never submitted. The Panda retrieval pool
   was the segment-extraction one prepared in late May / early June. We
   built the pool and pre-computed embeddings, but never launched the
   actual retrieval-augmented TTA sweep on Panda. **This is the only
   experiment that could give us a positive batch-retrieval result.**

2. **200v / 100v "small-N" comparison rows** — the user wants a side-by-side
   showing that early small-N gains compress at scale. Current discovery
   sweeps (`panda_cover_candidates`, `panda_adasteer_ablation`,
   `panda_longctx`, `ucf500_lora_collapse_cover`, `ucf_longctx`) range from
   N=26 to N=100, none at exactly N=200. We can either present those as-is
   or skip the small-N table. See `ANALYSIS_LOG.md` for the discussion.

3. **`AS_CLIP_T10` / `AS_CLIP_T15` ablations** — small-N panda discovery
   runs that haven't been promoted to 1000v.
