## How to read

- A falling `dynamic_degree` **mean** (fraction of clips RAFT calls dynamic) is the freeze trend. Median 0 in every window means most clips were already still.
- `imaging_quality` / `aesthetic_quality` drifting after piece 0 is where search actually acts (piece 0 is shared).
- Do not replace the full-clip VBench++ table with any one window.
