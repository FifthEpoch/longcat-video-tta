## Findings

1. **Learned config routing does not scale.** PSNR router capture 7.2%→3.3% (+0.054→+0.013 dB, ≈ fixed); the oracle headroom itself shrinks (+0.75→+0.38 dB).
2. **The pilot's 20.8% VBench-router capture was a small-N artifact.** At N=998 every learned VBench router (9–159 features) captures **negative** (best −1.6%). VBench routing is counterproductive.
3. **The grid is metrically redundant at scale** (pick agreement 39%, realized ρ 0.999): routers collapse onto a few near-equivalent configs (S5_LR1e3 dominant).
4. **The robust AdaSteer win is distributional, not routed:** fixed AdaSteer FVD 68.8 ≪ NOTTA 157.0. Oracle PSNR routing still co-improves SSIM/LPIPS (+0.0085 / −0.0108) but is not learnable from these features.
